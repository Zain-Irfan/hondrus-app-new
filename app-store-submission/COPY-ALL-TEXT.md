## 13. Data Safety Form (Google Play — copy these answers)

Google asks what data you collect. Use these answers:

- **Personal info collected:** Name, Email, Phone, Address — Yes, required for orders
- **Financial info:** No (Stripe handles all payment data; you don't store card numbers)
- **Location:** No
- **Web browsing:** No
- **App activity:** Yes — App interactions (for analytics)
- **Device IDs:** No
- **Data encrypted in transit:** Yes
- **Users can request deletion:** Yes — they email support@hondurasgrocers.com
- **Data shared with third parties:** Clerk (auth), Stripe (payments), Google Maps (address autocomplete), shipping carriers (UPS/FedEx)

---

## 14. App Store Connect — "App Privacy" Questionnaire (Apple)

Same data as above. For each category Apple asks, mark:
