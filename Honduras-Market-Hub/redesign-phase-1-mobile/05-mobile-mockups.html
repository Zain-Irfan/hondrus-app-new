Checkout & confirmation

Three-step progressive checkout (Envío → Pago → Revisar) using native Stripe PaymentSheet for Apple Pay / Google Pay / cards. Order success replaces the static tracking row with a 5-stop status timeline + a referral card at the moment of peak happiness.
